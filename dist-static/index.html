Our Philosophy

Beauty with simplicity.

Living walls couple beauty with simplicity to create healthy, living works of art. They are composed of a variety of tropical plants grown hydroponically.

Each living wall is custom made to satisfy our clients' dreams. We believe that bringing nature indoors should feel effortless — a seamless extension of your space and your vision.

Maintenance & Guarantee

100% plant guarantee — with maintenance.

A living wall is a living thing. Our maintenance program keeps every wall thriving — and every wall on an active maintenance agreement is covered by our 100% plant guarantee. If a plant fails, we replace it. No charge, no questions. The guarantee is available exclusively with maintenance service.

Since 2011, we have stood behind every wall we have created.

100% Plant Guarantee

Any plant that declines or dies is replaced at no cost for the life of your maintenance agreement.

Scheduled Maintenance

Regular visits for pruning, feeding, pest management, irrigation checks, and system calibration.

Plant Warranty Terms

Coverage stays active as long as maintenance is current. Without a maintenance plan, the guarantee does not apply.
