Sustainability

Every drop, used again.

Our hydroponic and aquaponic walls run on closed-loop recirculating water. Instead of draining away, the same water cycles through the root matrix again and again — topping up only what the plants actually drink. The result is a living wall that uses a fraction of the water of traditional irrigation while keeping roots oxygenated and healthy.

up to 90%

Water Reused

Closed-loop recirculation returns nearly all the water the roots don't drink straight back into the system.

0.35 L

Per Day, Per Square Foot

Typical daily top-up — only the small amount the plants actually transpire, not the full reservoir.

zero

Runoff to Drain

No municipal water wasted. What isn't taken up by the roots stays in the loop and is filtered for the next pass.
