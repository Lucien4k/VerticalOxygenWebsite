§ 3

Maintenance & Plant Guarantee

All living wall installations require a scheduled maintenance agreement to remain under plant warranty. The 100% plant guarantee is issued in conjunction with an active maintenance contract.

Since 2011, we have stood behind every wall we have created.

Maintenance Scope

Pruning & grooming
Every scheduled visit
Nutrient dosing
Reservoir feed adjustment
Pest management (IPM)
Every visit we inspect foliage undersides and the root zone for spider mite, aphid, mealybug, scale and fungus gnat. Issues are treated first with biological and low-toxicity controls — beneficial predatory insects, insecticidal soap, horticultural oil and neem — applied as targeted spot treatments rather than blanket spraying, so the space stays safe for occupants. Affected plants are isolated or replaced under the plant guarantee, and the same area is re-checked on the following visit until the population is cleared.
